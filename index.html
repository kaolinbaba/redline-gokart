How to play

Three steps to the grid

  1. STEP 1

    Drop your coin

    Pick your package, your crew size and a time. Booking takes about as long as dropping a coin.

  2. STEP 2

    Strap on your helmet

    A short safety briefing, a helmet and a balaclava. When the lights go green, the track is yours.

  3. STEP 3

    Write your story

    Every lap time hits the screen. Put your best lap on the leaderboard and leave the rematch to your friends.
